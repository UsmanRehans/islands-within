# Experience and Content Guide

## Brand

**Name:** Islands Within  
**Tagline:** Make space for your own voice.  
**Short description:** A private, creative reflection space where you build an inner archipelago, connect unexpected meanings, and notice what matters to you.

## Voice

Warm, spacious, curious, and non-judgmental. Speak beside the user, never above them. Prefer “might,” “notice,” “if you wish,” and “to you.” Avoid “This means…,” “You are…,” “Your result shows…,” or any diagnostic language.

## Landing page copy

### Hero

**Make space for your own voice.**

Build an island for what belongs to you. Place the people, places, and possibilities you want nearby. Then follow the meanings that emerge.

**Primary action:** Begin my archipelago  
**Secondary action:** See how it works

Private on this device · No artistic skill needed · Pause anytime

### Three-step preview

1. **Create** — Shape your Home Island and fill it with whatever is in your heart.
2. **Connect** — Add the islands around you and name the qualities they hold.
3. **Discover** — Pair what you carry until one personally made meaning remains.

## Opening reassurance

This is not about drawing well or making something beautiful. Use stick figures, simple shapes, words, or symbols. There are no right or wrong answers, and no one will evaluate your drawing. What matters is the meaning it holds for you.

## Gathering copy

Opens the Meaning Maker. Every named item appears as a shell; the user chooses which
to carry in.

**Not everything needs to come with you.**  
Choose what you want to carry into this. Most people carry twelve to sixteen.

If fewer than six are chosen: “You can carry as few as you like — though with only a
handful, there won’t be many connections to find. Would you like to add more first?”
Offer **Add more** and **Continue anyway**. Never block.

## Meaning Maker instructions

“Your shells will be paired at random. For each pair, create one shared title or meaning that can hold them both. There is no perfect answer. Continue combining until one final meaning remains.”

There is no reshuffle. If a user asks for one, the honest answer belongs in the copy:
“The order is part of what you were given today.”

Helpful prompts:

- What can hold both of these?
- What tension or harmony lives between them?
- If they belonged to one story, what might its title be?
- What becomes visible only when they sit together?

Odd item choice:

“One shell is unpaired. Would you like it to rest until the next round, or join a group of three?”

Final meaning:

“This is not an answer assigned to you. It is a meaning you made.”

## Reflection prompts

Before answering the questions, write three sentences that use qualities you identified in your archipelago.

1. What are the main elements you included on your island?
2. To what extent do you need other people?
3. Is there communication between the islands?
4. Are your basic needs available on your island?
5. What is most important to you?
6. How large is your island compared with the other islands?
7. Who is on your island, and who is on the other islands?
8. What forms of communication connect the islands?

### Optional deeper reflection

- What is taking up so much space inside me that there is not enough room for my own voice?
- What fear have I been allowing to make decisions for me?
- What expectations have I internalized?
- What guilt have I confused with love?
- What responsibility have I taken on that may belong to someone else?
- What might become possible if I stayed deeply connected to the people I love while becoming psychologically free?

Per-question controls: **Write a response** · **I’m not ready to answer this** · **Return later**

## Completion copy

Final sentence starter: “The space I want to protect is…”

## Privacy copy

“Your work is saved only in this browser on this device unless you choose to export it. Islands Within does not analyze, score, or upload your reflections.”

Export warning: “Your map may contain private names and reflections. Review what is included before downloading or sharing.”

Delete confirmation: “Delete this archipelago and all reflections from this device? This cannot be undone unless you exported a backup.”

## Gentle support note

“Reflection can sometimes bring up strong feelings. Pause if you need to. Consider reaching out to someone you trust or a qualified professional for support. This experience is educational and reflective; it is not a substitute for mental-health care or emergency help.”

## Optional “Why reflection?” drawer

Some moments invite us to pause, observe, question, understand, and then choose. This exercise makes room between another person’s fear and your own; between their disappointment and your decision; between guilt and responsibility; and between loving someone and feeling responsible for regulating their emotions.

For people of faith, reflection may also be a spiritual practice. The Qur’an repeatedly invites people to notice signs within and around themselves, to reason, and to reflect deeply. Offer this as an optional source of inspiration, nothing more — the exercise makes no spiritual claim of its own.
